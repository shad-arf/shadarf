Getting Started

What is Bazrgan System?

This system helps you manage your business by tracking money coming in (sales), money going out (expenses), and showing you how much profit you're making. Think of it as your business calculator and record keeper.

First Time Setup (5 minutes)
1
Download & Install (Recommended)

Visit https://app.bazrgan.com/bazrganapp/ to download the application. Install it on your computer for the best experience.

2
Login to Your System

Open the installed application and go to your system. Use the username and password given to you by your system administrator.

3
Check Your Dashboard

After login, you'll see the main page called "Dashboard". This shows you a summary of your business performance.

4
Look at the Left Menu

On the left side, you'll see a menu with options like Sales, Purchases, Items, Expenses. These are the tools you'll use every day.

Tip: Start by just looking around. You can't break anything by clicking and exploring! The desktop app is faster and works even when your internet is slow.

Daily Tasks - What to Do Every Day

Morning (5 minutes)
  • Open your Bazrgan app
  • Check your dashboard to see yesterday's results
  • Look at your Safe to see how much cash you have
  • Check if there are any new orders to process
During the Day
1
Record Every Sale

When you sell something, go to "Sales" in the left menu and add the sale details. This keeps track of your income.

2
Record Expenses

When you spend money (electricity, rent, supplies), go to "Expenses" and record it. This helps you see where money goes.

3
Update Inventory

If you buy new products, go to "Purchases" and record them. If you sell products, the system automatically reduces your stock.

End of Day (10 minutes)
  • Check your dashboard to see today's profit
  • Make sure all sales and expenses were recorded
  • Look at tomorrow's schedule if you have orders
  • If using desktop app, let it sync with the server
Important: Record transactions as soon as they happen. Don't wait until end of day - you might forget! With the desktop app, your data is saved locally and will sync when you're online.

Understanding Your Reports

What the Numbers Mean
Green Numbers (Good)
  • Sales: Money you earned
  • Net Income: Your profit
  • Cash: Money you have
  • Customer Payments: People paying you
Red Numbers (Costs)
  • Expenses: Money you spent
  • Purchases: Products you bought
  • Supplier Payments: Money you owe suppliers
Simple Business Health Check
  • Profitable Business: Net Income is positive (green number)
  • Good Cash Flow: Safe amount is increasing over time
  • Healthy Growth: Sales are higher than last month
Weekly Review (15 minutes every Friday)
  1. Open your Bazrgan app
  2. Change dashboard filter to "Week"
  3. Check if Net Income is positive
  4. Compare with last week - are you improving?
  5. Look at expenses - any unexpected costs?
  6. Plan for next week based on the numbers

Common Problems & Solutions

Why this happens: You haven't recorded any transactions for the selected time period.

How to fix:
  1. Change the time filter (try "All" instead of "Day")
  2. Make sure you've been recording your sales and expenses
  3. Check if you're looking at the right section
  4. If using desktop app, ensure it has synced recently

Common causes:
  • Forgot to record some transactions
  • Entered wrong amounts
  • Looking at wrong time period
  • Data hasn't synced yet (desktop app)
What to do:
  1. Double-check your recent entries
  2. Compare with your physical records
  3. Check sync status if using desktop app
  4. Contact support if still confused

Desktop app issues:
  • Try restarting the application
  • Check your internet connection
  • Contact IT support for login help
Need Help? Don't hesitate to call support. It's better to ask than to make mistakes with your business data!

Helpful Tips for Success

Best Practices
  • Use the desktop app for better performance
  • Keep the application open during business hours
  • Record transactions immediately
  • Check your dashboard every morning
  • Review weekly reports every Friday
  • Keep your device updated
  • Have reliable internet for syncing
  • Know your support contact
Things to Avoid
  • Don't close the app without letting it sync
  • Don't delay entering data
  • Don't ignore negative numbers
  • Don't forget to categorize expenses
  • Don't change system settings
  • Don't share your login details
  • Don't work offline for too long without syncing
  • Don't use multiple devices simultaneously
Monthly Success Routine

End of every month (30 minutes):

  1. Open your Bazrgan app
  2. Set dashboard to "Month" view
  3. Write down your Net Income
  4. Compare with last month
  5. Identify your biggest expenses
  6. Set goals for next month
  7. Celebrate if you made profit! 🎉
