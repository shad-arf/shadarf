Project Overview

Solar Sales Project is a Laravel business application built to manage inventory, sales, purchases, suppliers, customers, and financial records in one place. The system was designed to reduce scattered record-keeping and make daily business activity easier to monitor.

I handled the full-stack work needed to connect operational workflows, reporting, and document generation into a single application that supports practical sales management.

Key Features

  • Dashboard reporting for daily business visibility.
  • Low-stock tracking and inventory adjustment workflows.
  • Profit monitoring and PDF invoice export.
  • Centralized sales, purchasing, and record management.
