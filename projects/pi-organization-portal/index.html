Project Overview

Pi Organization Portal was designed as an official learning management and content platform. I handled the design and development of the portal, covering student registration, lecture uploads, certificate generation, and role-based access for students, teachers, and administrators.

The project also included FastPay integration for payments and reporting tools for operational oversight, making it more than a simple content site and closer to a full online learning system.

Key Features

  • Course management, lecture uploads, and certificate generation.
  • Role management for students, teachers, and administrators.
  • FastPay integration and payment status monitoring.
  • Relational schema with 16+ tables and admin reporting tools.
