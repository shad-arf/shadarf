Project Overview

The Soran University Career Development Center platform was built to support CDC events and related operations online. I contributed as a PHP developer, working on CRUD features, event management functionality, and dynamic dashboards that gave staff a better way to manage programs and content.

A key part of the work was translating staff requirements into practical features that were easy to use and aligned with how the center operated day to day.

Key Features and Contributions

  • Built and maintained CRUD-driven platform features.
  • Implemented event management tools for CDC programs and activities.
  • Added dynamic dashboards for operational visibility.
  • Collaborated directly with staff to turn workflow needs into working features.
